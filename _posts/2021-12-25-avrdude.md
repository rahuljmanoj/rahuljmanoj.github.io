<h2>What is AVRDUDE?</h2>

<p>AVRDUDE (AVR Downloader Uploader) is a versatile command-line tool used to upload and download <code>.hex</code> files and manage memory on Atmel AVR microcontrollers.</p>

<p>With AVRDUDE, you can:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Read/write flash memory</li>
  <li>Access EEPROM</li>
  <li>Modify fuse and lock bits</li>
  <li>Check device signature</li>
</ul>

<p>It also offers a terminal mode for interactive control and debugging of your microcontroller memory and fuse configurations.</p>

<h2>Basic AVRDUDE Commands</h2>

<p>I typically use the <strong>usbasp</strong> programmer with an <strong>Atmega8</strong> as the target MCU:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Check device connectivity:</strong><br>
    <code>avrdude -p m8 -c usbasp</code>
  </li>
  <li><strong>Enter terminal mode:</strong><br>
    <code>avrdude -p m8 -c usbasp -t</code>
  </li>
  <li><strong>Write fuse bits:</strong><br>
    <code>avrdude -p m8 -c usbasp -u -U hfuse:w:0xDF:m -U lfuse:w:0xEF:m</code>
  </li>
  <li><strong>Upload flash firmware:</strong><br>
    <code>avrdude -p m8 -c usbasp -u -U flash:w:filename.hex</code>
  </li>
  <li><strong>Write EEPROM:</strong><br>
    <code>avrdude -p m8 -c usbasp -u -U eeprom:w:filename.hex</code>
  </li>
</ul>

---

<h2>Practical Notes</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Replace <code>m8</code> with your target MCU (e.g., <code>atmega328p</code>).</li>
  <li>Replace <code>usbasp</code> with your programmer type if different.</li>
  <li>Replace <code>filename.hex</code> with your compiled hex file.</li>
</ul>

<p>AVRDUDE is a must-have tool for serious AVR developers and hobbyists, allowing you full control over your microcontroller’s configuration and firmware deployment efficiently.</p>
