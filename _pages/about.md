Thank you for visiting my profile 🙂

I am an electrical engineer and interdisciplinary research scientist working at the intersection of biomedical instrumentation, sensing technologies, and cardiovascular health. 
My work focuses on building reliable, user-focused systems that translate complex measurements into clinically meaningful insights.

Currently, I am a [Postdoctoral Research Fellow](https://www.ntnu.edu/employees/rahul.manoj) with the [Division of Biomechanics](https://www.ntnu.edu/biomechanics), [Department of Structural Engineering](https://www.ntnu.no/kt) at [NTNU](https://www.ntnu.no), Norway, where I collaborate with academia, hospitals, and industry on physics-based and data-driven modelling, credibility asessment of computational models, and build digital twin solutions for cardiovascular health diagnosis and monitoring.

With over seven years of experience in applied R&D, I have worked across electro-mechanical systems, electronic system design, ultrasound technologies, multi-modality sensing, signal processing, algorithm development, physiological modelling, and cybernectics, building functional prototypes for V&V studies.

I enjoy working in multidisciplinary environments where I traslate applied engineering research into impactfull products. I am now excited to transition into roles where I can contribute to translational research, algorithm development, technology development, system design, and product engineering.

If you'd like to connect, collaborate, or discuss opportunities, feel free to reach out to me.
